# Approved rules (public summary)
This page is a plain-language summary written by hand from the League's design document, checked against the edition and hash shown above. Where the document and this page differ, the document wins. Only approved rules are listed.

## The league
Forty-eight clubs in two conferences (Western and Eastern), four divisions per conference, six clubs per division. In each division five clubs are active and one is the Ambassador for the year, so forty clubs play the regular season and eight are Ambassadors.
Each club plays 18 regular-season games, nine at home and nine away, over 19 numbered weeks, with three breaks (after weeks 4, 9 and 14) and one bye per club in weeks 15 to 19.

## Standings and tiebreaks
Win percentage is wins divided by wins plus losses; a tied game counts as not played for wins, losses and opponent lists but its points still count toward points scored, points allowed and point differential.
Ties are broken by one cascade, used for divisions, playoff seeding, exile, the draft and the Week 4 conference comparison: overall wins; overall win percentage; division wins; division win percentage; conference wins; conference win percentage; point differential; strength of schedule; strength of victory; a seeded draw. Division and conference steps apply only when every tied club is in that division or conference. There is no head-to-head step.

## Exile and seeds
After the last regular-season game, each division's five active clubs are ranked. Ranks 1 to 4 become next season's seeds 1 to 4. The last-place club is exiled and becomes next season's Ambassador; exile cannot be declined. The current Ambassador returns as seed 5. A division's last-place club cannot take a wild card.

## Playoffs
Six clubs per conference: the four division winners (seeds 1 to 4) and the two best other clubs (seeds 5 and 6). Seeds 1 and 2 have a bye. Wild Card round: seed 3 hosts seed 6 and seed 4 hosts seed 5. Divisional round is reseeded: seed 1 hosts the lowest surviving seed and seed 2 hosts the other survivor. In the Conference Championship the higher remaining seed hosts. Playoff games cannot end in a tie.

## The Diamond Coronation
The two conference champions meet in the Diamond Coronation, the last Sunday of the year, always at Amethyst Aloha Stadium and always on neutral terms: neither finalist has a home-field edge. After Week 4 the league compares the two conferences on their games against each other and names which conference's finalist is the nominal home club; that decision is made once and not changed.
The Coronation score is kept in the season's permanent record. For a season played before that record existed, the page shows its score only when the league's game archive holds the game, and otherwise says the score was not recorded.

## The Ambassador Tour and Bowl
The eight Ambassadors play a seven-round, 28-game single round robin at international venues. Each round is played at one venue, and no venue is any club's home ground. The nominal home club kicks the first half.
On the Sunday before the Coronation, Western and Eastern Ambassador All-Stars meet in the Ambassador Bowl at its own venue. Each side is drawn from its conference's four Ambassador clubs: the eight Ambassador head coaches rank the players by position on ranked ballots, with 48 positional places and 5 discretionary places. All 53 selected players dress. Each side is coached by its conference's best Tour club. The ballots are not published.

## Rosters and the salary cap
A club has a 53-player roster and a 16-player practice squad (at most six of them veterans with more than two credited seasons); the preseason ceiling is 90 including the practice squad. A club must keep at least the positional minimum at each position, and must be able to field 11 medically available players or forfeit.
The salary cap is $1,000 (in the League's units), with an emergency reserve of $25 and a minimum salary of $5. Unused cap room does not carry over. Contracts run up to five years and are steady, rising or declining; signing bonuses are spread evenly over the contract's remaining years, to a maximum of five. Dead money counts against the cap.

## The draft and rookies
Seven rounds of 48 picks. Rookies sign four-year contracts at fixed scale salaries ($25, $18, $14, $11, $9, $7 and $5 for rounds 1 to 7). A first-round contract carries a club option on a fifth year at $35; an exercised option is fully guaranteed.

## The game
Four 15-minute quarters; 40-second play clock; three timeouts per half; no coin toss (home kicks first half, away the second). Touchdown 6, field goal 3, safety 2, extra point 1, two-point tries always available. Regular-season and Tour overtime is one 10-minute period in which both teams get a possession; postseason overtime continues in 15-minute periods.

## Not yet decided or not yet built
Contract extensions, midseason contract rules, and some details of the Ambassador Bowl count still await the owner's rulings and are not listed here.
