# Baseline defects: founding, free agency, depth signings (work record, 2026-11-22)

Package authorized 2026-11-22 05:23 PST. Branch `fix/baseline-founding-fa-2026-11-22`, from the baseline `4a1f26e`, independent of the Sacramento branch (which stays deferred and unmerged). Local code, tests and documentation only. Every league here is disposable, on seeds reserved for testing (7771000-7771999); no competitive outcome was measured or is reported. The viewing league was not selected, inspected or advanced.

## 1. Founding can produce a cap-illegal club: CONFIRMED, unintended, FIXED

* **Reproduction.** `Universe(tables, seed=7771413)` raises `Rejected: sign: breaks the cap for Tampa Bay` while founding (`World._found`, a practice-squad signing). Same on `4a1f26e`. Frequency: 1 of 60 test seeds screened (7771400-7771459); the first 32 seeds of the Sacramento comparison all founded.
* **Cause.** Founding signs each club's 53 with a rookie contract whose scale round is drawn uniformly at random (`rng.randint(1, 7)`, salaries 25 to 5), then 16 squad players at 5. Nothing checks the cap. A club's payroll averages about 810 and its draw has a long tail; on rare seeds a club has drawn so many early rounds that it cannot sign its squad within 1,000 (here 997 held with the ninth squad player still to sign).
* **Documented rule or accident?** The design says every club starts with an all-rookie 53 and a squad of 16 (§11 Founding Day, `docs/ROADMAP.md`) and that every signing is cap-checked by the ledger. Nothing says a club may be founded over the cap or that league creation may fail. Unintended.
* **Fix.** `World._founding_contract`: the drawn round stands unless the club could then no longer sign the signings still to come, each at least the league minimum, within the cap; then the first round at or after the one drawn that fits is used. The random draw is made exactly as before, so **every seed that founded before founds identically** (tested: three seeds' founding digests equal those produced by `4a1f26e`; a further 120-event digest identity was verified earlier for the unmodified paths). Only a club that could not be founded legally is touched, and its contracts remain ordinary rookie-scale contracts.
* **Tests** (`tests/test_baseline_defects.py`): the failing seed founds with every club at 53/16 and at or under the cap; every founding contract is a four-year rookie-scale contract with no bonus; unchanged digests for seeds that founded before; the rule itself, case by case (a round that fits is kept, otherwise the first that fits, and the cheapest when signings are still to come). The tests were committed failing first (`git log`), then the fix.

## 2. Free agency ends a club's whole period on one empty position: CONFIRMED, unintended, FIXED

* **Reproduction.** The free-agency loop draws a needed position at random; if no free agent at that position is one the club rates above 60 it executed `break`, ending the club's entire period with budget, roster room and other needs untouched. About 29% of club-years ended this way. A test makes every club need two positions, one with no candidate, and always draws the empty one first: on `4a1f26e` 45 of 45 clubs signed nobody in the loop.
* **Documented rule or accident?** The spec describes free agency as filling template shortfalls (design item 38: "free agency alone fills only template shortfalls") and states there is no salary floor (§11, owner's ruling 2026-10-22). Neither describes ending a club's period because of the draw. The spending target (cap less reserve less 90), the 40-pass limit, the roster ceiling and the "better than 60" bar are builder defaults in the autopilot, not rules, and are **not changed**. The `break` is an implementation shortcut. Unintended.
* **Fix.** A position with no suitable candidate is recorded as empty for that club's period and another position is drawn; the period ends when no position the club could draw has a candidate (and the other existing stop conditions are untouched). Empty draws do not count against the 40-pass limit (the limit still counts signings tried), so the limit is not changed in effect. Leagues where no club ever drew an empty position are unchanged; in practice nearly every league changes after its first free agency, because about 29% of club-years used to end this way.
* **Tests:** the case above (every club that could sign at the other position now does, and a club that signs nobody has no taker left at it; supply is limited so some clubs legitimately find nobody); the period still terminates when no position has a candidate.
* **Not changed:** scouting accuracy, negotiation, the camp upgrade limits, the roster ceiling, the spending target, game resolution.

## 3. Free-agency signings at filled positions ("below the incumbent"): INVESTIGATED, NOT a defect on this evidence, not changed

When no position is short the loop draws a template position at random and signs the best free agent there (by the club's own view). The earlier note that 55% of such signings were below the incumbent starter compared only against the starter and came from one club. A measurement over all 48 clubs (6 disposable seeds, 3 league years, 6,640 such signings, free-agency fix in place) classifies each signing, at the moment she is signed, by the club's own value (view with age counted):

| Class | Meaning | Share | Cut at final cuts | Guaranteed money plus bonus of those cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter upgrade | beats the club's weakest starter at the position | 60% | under 1% | 42 in total |
| Makes the template | not a starter upgrade, but ranks within the club's template count at the position (depth that makes the 53) | 24% | 6% | 387 in total |
| Below the template | ranks below every player the template keeps at the position | 16% | 58% | 2,023 in total (about 3.4 per player cut) |

* **Depth is legitimate.** Clubs keep players for injuries, special teams and competition at camp; a signing that beats the position's depth cut line is reasonable. 84% of the signings are an upgrade or make the template, so "below the incumbent" is mostly depth, and the rule that every signing improve the starter is neither in the spec nor wanted.
* **Price:** about 4 in 10 of the below-template signings are made at the league minimum; those cut cost nothing; the guarantees and bonuses carried by the rest average about 3 per player cut against a cap of 1,000.
* **Potential:** the club's valuation (`World.value`) is the view of current overall less an age penalty after 30. Development and potential are not part of it, so they cannot be what justifies a signing; this is the current model, not a defect.
* **Roster needs:** a needy position is filled first; extras are drawn only once none is short.
* **What is left is an efficiency question, not a legality or money one:** the 16% below the template use roster-plus-squad places (the loop's ceiling is 81) for players the club will mostly cut, which can crowd out later signings in the same period. It is the same order of effect as the other autopilot behaviours and is a design choice.
* **Recommendation (one approach, not implemented):** leave it. If a tighter autopilot is wanted later, draw a filled position only when the best candidate would rank within the club's template count at that position (skip the draw otherwise). It changes league dynamics across the board and needs its own decision and a validation cycle; it is not a defect fix.

## 4. Other observations (not changed)

* Failed negotiations still count against the 40-pass limit; the camp upgrade routine remains limited to 3 signings and to the best free agent per position. Both are rules or builder defaults, not touched here.

## 5. Tests and validation impact

* **New tests:** `tests/test_baseline_defects.py` (6 tests), committed failing first, passing after the fixes (checked by running the committed failing version against the fixed code's parent).
* **Regression run:** every `tests/test_*.py` file (118 files, about 1,800 tests, the CI discovery command run file by file in two parallel processes). All pass except four tests that are the **pin gates by design** and fail on any change to the code under test: `test_alert_release_config` (2: "the code under test differs from the pin" and the package/pin-gate classification), `test_replay_tools` (1: the deployed-release code fingerprint) and `test_vtest` (1: "the code under test has not changed since the pin"). All four pass on `4a1f26e`. Three files whose output ended with log noise were re-run for a clean result (`test_dfl_alert` 46 OK, `test_rehearsal_guard` 25 OK; `test_vtest` as above). Skips are the existing environment skips.
* **Structural effect of the free-agency fix** (3 disposable seeds, 3 league years, engine off; payroll and roster counts only, no competitive outcome): mean payroll at camp opening 867 before, 879 after (about +1.3%); club-years still below the spending target at camp 160 before, 113 after (about -30%); mean roster plus squad 81 in both. The audit and cap checks of the existing tests held.
* **Validation impact: the candidate changes, so the frozen configuration cannot carry these fixes.** The edit is in `src/dfl/ledger/world.py` (code). `tools/vtest/PIN` and `pin-check` correctly fail on this branch; `vtest.py affected --recorded 4a1f26e` names V0 to V5 for repeat (the harness is conservative: any code change counts). Adopting the fixes needs a new candidate, a new pin and a validation cycle. The founding fix leaves every seed that founded before byte-identical; the free-agency fix changes league evolution after the first free agency in almost every league. Nothing here modifies `tools/vtest`, `alerts` or the pin; production and the viewing league were not touched.
* **Launch note:** until the fixes are adopted, any launch seed must be created first to confirm it founds (about 2 seeds in 100 fail on the baseline).
